1. Executive Overview & Architecture

The Handicrafts Association of Bhutan platform operates an end-to-end synchronized architecture connecting the Secretariat Admin Panel (/admin), a robust PostgreSQL database (Prisma ORM), and the Public Website (Next.js App Router). Every piece of public information—including event listings, donation pillars, master artisan honours, membership dues, catalog items, and logistics terms—is dynamically driven by the database with real-time administrative controls and resilient default safety fallbacks.

3. In-Depth Operational Breakdown by Module
1. Site Settings & Global CMS /admin/site-settings
Operations Supported READUPDATE
Database & APIs Model: SiteSettingPUT /api/admin/site-settings

Organized into specialized tabs: General (taglines, statistics), Contact (addresses, phone numbers, P.O. Box), About Page (official mandate, history, objectives, values), Wholesale (MOQs, lead times, 5 assurance cards), Checkout (BoB official account, SWIFT, branch, mBOB notes), Donate (fundraising lede, tax exemption notes), and Logistics (carrier partners, transit times).

Public Frontend Impact Directly feeds Homepage (/), About (/about), Contact (/contact), Wholesale (/wholesale), Checkout (/checkout), Order Confirmation (/order-confirmation/[orderNumber]), and the global footer.
2. Events & Exhibitions Management /admin/events
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Model: EventRecordCRUD /api/admin/events

Controls exhibition titles, categories (Exhibition, Masterclass, Trade Expo, National Festival), dates, venues, organizers, craft disciplines, schedule timelines, and registration buttons.

Public Frontend Impact Immediately populates the filterable directory on /events, dynamically provisions dedicated event pages on /events/[key], and updates the calendar on /news.
3. Donate Settings & Contributions Ledger /admin/donate-settings
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Models: SupportPillar, DonationRecordCRUD /api/admin/support-pillars

Allows administrators to define fundraising pillars (target amounts, raised totals, icon badges, descriptions) and review the immutable audit ledger of all public donor contributions.

Public Frontend Impact The public donation page on /donate renders live funding progress bars. Online donations generate official receipts (HAB-DON-YYYY-XXXXX) and increment pillar totals in real-time.
4. Honours & Master Artisans /admin/honours
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Model: HonourRecordCRUD /api/admin/honours

Manages national craft accreditations, conferred master artisans, Royal Seal recipients, Seal of Authenticity awardees, citations, dzongkhags, and portraits.

Public Frontend Impact Populates the Master Artisans gallery on /masters, filterable by award type and traditional craft specialization.
5. Membership Categories & Dues /admin/membership-categories
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Model: MembershipCategoryCRUD /api/admin/membership-categories

Maintains guild tiers (Individual, Enterprise, Group, Supporter), annual dues in BTN (Nu.) and USD, eligibility criteria, registration steps, and member entitlements.

Public Frontend Impact Renders the "Five ways to belong" comparative matrix on /membership, generates dedicated tier landing pages on /membership/[category], and dynamically calculates dues on /register.
6. Clusters & Physical Outlets /admin/clusters-outlets
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Models: ClusterRecord, OutletRecordCRUD /api/admin/clusters, .../outlets

Maintains registered artisan clusters across Bhutan (member counts, village histories, visitor notes) and physical craft markets (Punakha Crafts Market, opening hours, facilities, directions).

Public Frontend Impact Feeds /clusters, /clusters/[key], /outlets, /outlets/[key], and the cluster associations on /craft/[craft].
7. Product Inventory & Wholesale B2B /admin/products
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETECSV IMPORT/EXPORT
Database & APIs Models: Product, WholesaleTierCRUD /api/admin/products

Controls retail and trade inventory: product codes (e.g. THAG-001), USD & BTN pricing, artisan attribution, origin dzongkhag, materials, photography, stock levels, and wholesale tiered pricing against MOQs.

Public Frontend Impact Drives the retail shop (/shop), product details (/product/[code]), wholesale purchasing (/wholesale/shop), and the administrative POS checkout (/admin/pos).
8. Orders, Fulfillment & Tracking /admin/orders
Operations Supported READSTATUS UPDATEASSIGN TRACKING
Database & APIs Models: Order, OrderItemGET, PUT /api/admin/orders

Enables Secretariat staff to transition orders through fulfillment stages (Pending, Paid, Processing, Shipped, Delivered), attach international Bhutan Post EMS tracking numbers, and download invoices.

Public Frontend Impact Updates the public shipment tracking portal on /track-order and the receipt tracking view on /order-confirmation/[orderNumber].
9. Statutory Content, Policies & Publications /admin/content
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEDELETE
Database & APIs Models: PolicyPage, Publication, NewsArticleCRUD /api/admin/content

Complete content management for statutory legal policies (Shipping, Terms, Privacy), downloadable executive publications (PDF Annual Reports, Strategic Plans), and newsroom articles.

Public Frontend Impact Renders live legal policies on /shipping, /terms, /privacy; downloads on /publications; and articles on /news and /news/[slug].
10. Security, RBAC & Immutable Audit Logs /admin/users & /admin/audit
Operations Supported CREATEREADUPDATEREVOKE SESSIONS
Database & APIs Models: User, Role, AuditLog, RateLimitAttemptCRUD /api/admin/users

Governs Secretariat staff logins, multi-factor authentication (2FA), role assignments (Super Admin, Staff Operator, Trustee Viewer), session revocations, and audit records of all administrative actions.

Operational Impact Secures all /admin/* routes behind JWT token checks. Enforces durable sliding-window rate limiting on public endpoints to prevent abuse.
4. Core Architecture Guarantees

1. DB-First with Resilient Fallback: Every public page queries the live PostgreSQL database first. If any table or record is ever empty, the system gracefully falls back to canonical default records (CLIENT_DATA), ensuring zero 404s, broken layouts, or blank screens.

2. Zero Hardcoded Content: All public headlines, body narratives, bank details, dues amounts, and logistics terms are 100% editable from the Admin Panel.

3. Strict Security & CSOA Compliance: All administrative modifications are permanently logged in the AuditLog table with actor ID, IP address, and timestamp in compliance with Civil Society Organizations Act of Bhutan (2007) guidelines.

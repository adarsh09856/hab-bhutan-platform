## 1. Logging In to the Admin Studio
1. Open your web browser and navigate to: `https://your-domain.com/admin` (or `http://localhost:3000/admin`).
2. Enter your authorized staff email address and password.
3. Upon logging in, you will see the **HAB Control Center** with 5 primary categories on the left sidebar:
   - **1. Website Pages**: Homepage, About Us, Programmes (A-K), Projects, Outlets, Crafts, News, Events, Publications, Donate.
   - **2. Shop & E-Commerce**: Products & Stock, Online Orders, Counter Billing (POS), Wholesale Sourcing.
   - **3. Artisans & Community**: Members Directory, Applications, Membership Categories.
   - **4. Settings & Customization**: Website Settings, Partner Logos, Menus & Links, Photo Library.
   - **5. Overview & Reports**: Sales Reports, System Status.

---

## 2. Visual Live Edit Mode (Instant Front-End Editing)
When logged in as staff, you have access to the **Visual Live Edit Mode**:
1. Visit any public page on the website (e.g. `/`, `/about`, `/news`, `/donate`, `/members`).
2. At the top of your screen, notice the dark **Admin Toolbar**.
3. Toggle the switch labeled **Visual Edit: ON**.
4. Every editable section will display an amber badge with a pencil icon (e.g. `[✏️ Edit Hero Slideshow]`, `[✏️ Edit Support Pillars]`, `[✏️ Edit News & Stories]`).
5. Click any badge to jump directly into the management studio for that exact section!

## 3. Managing Development Partners & Logo Artwork
To manage development partners (e.g. EU SWITCH-Asia, UNDP Bhutan, RGoB, SHINE):
1. Go to **Settings & Customization > Website Settings** (or **Website Pages > Homepage Studio > Partners tab**).
2. Click the **[+ Add Partner]** button.
3. In the pop-up window:
   - Enter the **Partner Organization Name** (e.g., `UNDP Bhutan`).
   - Enter their **Website URL** (e.g., `https://www.undp.org/bhutan`).
   - Click the upload area under **Partner Logo Artwork** to pick a transparent PNG, SVG, or WebP file.
4. Click **[Add to Showcase]**.
5. The logo immediately appears in the partner card grid.
6. Click **[Save Changes]** at the bottom of the screen. The logo is now live on the public homepage!

## 4. Uploading Photographs & Documents
Every studio features a user-friendly drag-and-drop file uploader:
- **Uploading an Image**: Click the upload box or drag an image directly from your computer. You will see an instant preview thumbnail.
- **Accepted Image Formats**: JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG (up to 30 MB).
- **Accepted Document Formats**: PDF (for annual reports, audited accounts, B2B wholesale catalogs).
- **Removing or Replacing**: Click **[Replace]** to pick a new photo, or **[Remove]** to clear the image.

## 5. Managing the Homepage Hero Slideshow
1. Go to **Website Pages > Homepage Studio** or **Hero Slideshow** (`/admin/hero`).
2. Click **[+ Add Slide]**.
3. Upload a high-resolution slide photo (recommended: 1200x800).
4. Enter a descriptive **Caption** (e.g., `Kushutara master weaver at traditional backstrap loom · Khoma, Lhuentse`).
5. Optionally set a destination link (e.g., `/shop` or `/outlets/punakha-market`).
6. Click **[Save Slide]**. Your changes reflect on the homepage carousel immediately.

## 6. Managing News Stories & Upcoming Events
- **Publishing a News Story**:
  1. Go to **Website Pages > News & Stories** (`/admin/content`).
  2. Click **[+ Add New Story]**.
  3. Enter Title, Category (Sector News, Feature, Notice), and publication date.
  4. Upload the **Cover Photo**.
  5. Use the rich text formatting toolbar to write your story.
  6. Click **[Publish Story]**.
- **Adding an Upcoming Event**:
  1. Go to **Website Pages > Exhibitions & Events** (`/admin/events`).
  2. Click **[+ Add Event]**.
  3. Set the Event Title, Venue (e.g. `Punakha Mo Chhu Riverbank`), Date, and Time.
  4. Upload promotional poster/banner artwork.
  5. Click **[Save Event]**.

---

## 7. Managing Members & Artisan Profiles
1. Go to **Artisans & Community > Artisan Directory** (`/admin/members`).
2. Click **[+ Add Artisan Member]** or click on an existing member to edit.
3. Enter their Full Name, Enterprise Name, Primary Craft (e.g. `Thagzo (Weaving)`), and Dzongkhag location.
4. Upload their **Artisan Portrait Avatar**.
5. Toggle their verification status badge (`Active Verified Member`).
6. Click **[Save Member]**.

---

## 8. Managing Products, E-Shop & Wholesale Catalog
- **Adding an E-Shop Product**:
  1. Go to **Shop & E-Commerce > Products & Stock** (`/admin/products`).
  2. Enter Product Code (e.g. `HAB-TEX-001`), Title, Craft category, and Price in Nu. BTN and USD $.
  3. Upload primary photo and multi-angle gallery photos.
  4. Set current stock count and artisan maker attribution.
  5. Click **[Save Product]**.
- **Updating Wholesale B2B Terms & Catalog**:
  1. Go to **Shop & E-Commerce > Wholesale Accounts & Orders** (`/admin/trade`).
  2. Switch to the **B2B Catalog & Media** tab.
  3. Upload the latest **Wholesale PDF Catalog** and **Lookbook Cover**.
  4. Set Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) and production lead times.

---

## 9. Fulfilling Customer Orders & POS Billing
1. Go to **Shop & E-Commerce > Online Orders** (`/admin/orders`).
2. View pending orders, customer shipping addresses, and selected payment method (Credit Card, mBoB, or Bank Transfer).
3. Once bank wire or mBoB receipt is verified, click **[Mark Paid]**.
4. When parcel is packed, enter the Bhutan Post EMS tracking number and click **[Mark Dispatched]**.
5. Customers can track their order in real-time at `/track-order`.

---

## 10. Updating Institutional Policies & Site Settings
- **Office Address & Contacts**: Update official phone numbers, ED contact, email, and secretariat location under **Settings & Customization > Website Settings**.
- **Statutory CSO Notice & Copyright**: Update legal credentials under the **Footer & Legal** tab.
- **Support Pillars**: Update donation funds and payment remittance details under **Website Pages > Donation Appeals**.
